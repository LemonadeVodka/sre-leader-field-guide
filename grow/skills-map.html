Use This When

You want an honest picture of where you stand as an SRE leader and one concrete thing to practice this week. Rate yourself once now (10 minutes), then re-rate each quarter.

How to Rate Honestly
  • 1 · Not Yet: you couldn't do it today without help.
  • 2 · With Help: you can do it if someone walks you through it or you prepare a lot.
  • 3 · Solid: you do it reliably, in the moment, without notes.
  • 4 · Can Teach It: you could coach a new manager through it.

Rate what you'd actually do on a bad Tuesday, not on your best day. Nobody else sees this.

Track Progress Over Time

Save a snapshot at the start of each quarter. Next quarter, re-rate and compare.

What Actually Happens
You rate yourself 3 on everything to feel better.
Counter: if you rated something 3, you should be able to name the last time you did it. If you can't, it's a 2.
You read about the skill instead of practicing it.
Counter: every skill has a "rep" that happens in a real meeting this week. Credibility comes from what people see you do, not what you've read.
The technical skills feel impossible, so you only work on the people ones.
Counter: the technical bar for you is "follow and steer the conversation," not "do the work." Pick one Technical Fluency skill each quarter, no matter what.
