SRE Leader's Field Guide

Lead reliability by asking the right questions. Start with the framework for the moment you're in. Every question is one you can ask without writing code.

Your Three Frameworks

One word for each moment. Each letter is a question you ask out loud. Tap a letter to go deeper, or print all three.

LEAD Running an incident

Join the bridge, then repeat every 15 minutes.

  1. LLeader. Who's leading this? If nobody, I'll lead.
  2. EEffect. What are users seeing, since when, getting worse?
  3. AAction. What's ruled out, best guess, safest step in 15 minutes?
  4. DDeadline. Who are we waiting on, when do we escalate, next update?

Stuck? Questions by how it's stuck

SAFER Postmortem or RCA

Follows the 45-minute postmortem agenda.

  1. SSet the rules. Blameless, facts first, owned actions.
  2. AAgree the impact. Who, how long, how did we find out?
  3. FFollow the timeline. What did you know, what made sense?
  4. EExplain what let it happen. Trigger, weak spots, why not caught?
  5. RRank the actions. If only one, which? Owner, date, done.

PACE Tracking people's work

Ask each owner, then write the E line down.

  1. PProgress. What moved since last time?
  2. AAsks. What's blocking you? Who's waiting on you?
  3. CConfidence. Date confidence 1 to 10? What makes it a 9?
  4. EEnd with owners. Who does what by when; I'll check on…
