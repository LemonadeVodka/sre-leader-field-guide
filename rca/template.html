RCA Template

A copy-ready RCA template, plus a one-page summary for execs.

How to Use It

  • Copy it into your team's doc tool. The incident lead or the owning pod's engineer fills it in. You review it with the 5-minute check.
  • Keep it short: a strong RCA is usually 2–3 pages.
  • Send the one-page summary below to leadership, support, and the NOC once actions are agreed.

One-Page Summary for Leadership

Exec summary
Incident summary: [title], [date], Sev [x]

What happened: [one sentence in user terms]
Impact: [% / number affected], [duration], [what users saw]
Why: [trigger] + [top 1-2 contributing factors]
Fixed by: [mitigation] at [time]

What we're changing:
1. [Top action]: [owner], [date]
2. [Detection action]: [owner], [date]
3. [Third action]: [owner], [date]

Ask / decision needed: [anything you need from leadership, or "none"]
Full RCA: [link]

Before You Mark an RCA "Done"

  • All actions have a person, a date, and a checkable definition of done.
  • Actions are in the tracker, and on the agenda for the monthly reliability review.
  • The summary went to leadership, support, and the NOC.
  • The top action has been verified to reduce the risk: a drill, a test, or watching the metric that failed.
