Check Incident response

Does Anyone Back Up the On-Call?

The situation
A Sev 2 at 02:00. The owning team's on-call doesn't answer, and their escalation policy has one person on it.
Do this today
Open PagerDuty. For your top 3 services, check each escalation policy. Write down any with no secondary or no manager step.
Say this
"[Pod lead], your policy for [service] has one person. Can we add a secondary by Friday?"
Check it worked
Each of the 3 has a secondary, or a named owner and a date to add one.
Why it makes you better
Most 'nobody answered' delays are visible in advance. Finding them before 2 a.m. is what great incident leaders do.
