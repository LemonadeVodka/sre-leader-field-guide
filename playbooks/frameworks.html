SRE Leader's Field Guide

Pocket Frameworks

Three one-word frameworks you can grab under pressure: LEAD for incidents, SAFER for postmortems and RCAs, and PACE for tracking people's work.

How to Use These

Three short frameworks for the moments that matter most. Each letter is one question you ask out loud. You don't need the technical answer; you need the next question. Print this page, or keep it open on a second screen.

LEAD: Running an Incident

Use it the moment you join a bridge, then run it again every 15 minutes until it's resolved. If you only remember one thing during an incident, remember LEAD.

LetterAskListen forGo deeper
L Leader"Who's leading this? If nobody, I'll lead until the owning team takes over."One name, and everyone on the call knows it.Nobody owns it
E Effect"What are users seeing, how many, since when, and is it getting worse?"One sentence with a number, a start time, and a direction.Impact is unclear
A Action"What have we ruled out, what's our best guess, and what's the safest thing we can do in the next 15 minutes?"A ruled-out list, a testable theory, and one action with an owner.Going in circles · Afraid to act
D Deadline"Who are we waiting on, when do we escalate if they don't answer, and when's the next update?"A name, a time, a backup, and an update time. Then you send the exec update.Waiting on someone · Exec heat

SAFER: Running a Postmortem or RCA

The goal of a postmortem is a safer system, so the framework is SAFER. It follows the 45-minute postmortem agenda, and works just as well when you're reading or judging an RCA document.

LetterAskListen forGo deeper
S Set the rules"Blameless: we're looking at how our system let this happen, not who. Facts first. We leave with owned actions."People relax. If anyone hears blame, they say so.Ground rules to copy
A Agree the impact"Who was affected, for how long, and how did we find out: us or customers?"Numbers everyone agrees with. Settle disagreement now.Running an RCA
F Follow the timeline"Walk us through it minute by minute. What did you know at that point, and what made sense to do?"Times from evidence (alerts, logs, chat), not memory.Worked example
E Explain what let it happen"What triggered it, what made us vulnerable, and why didn't we catch it sooner?"Several contributing factors and missing defenses. Never "human error" alone.RCA the SRE way
R Rank the actions"If we only did one, which? Who owns it, by when, and what does done mean?"3 to 5 ranked actions, each with one owner, a date, and a done-means.Judging an RCA

A  Agree the impact  Who was affected, for how long, how did we find out?
F  Follow timeline   Minute by minute: what did you know, what made sense?
E  Explain           What triggered it, what made us vulnerable, why not caught sooner?

PACE: Tracking People's Work

For any meeting where you need to know where work stands: the weekly sweep, a 1:1, the engineering block of the SRE Daily, or a task check-in. Ask each owner the four letters, then write the E line down.

LetterAskListen forGo deeper
P Progress"What moved since last time?"Something specific that's done, not "working on it."Decode the answer
A Asks"What's blocking you, or who are you waiting on? Is anyone waiting on you?"A named blocker you can clear. "Nothing" every week is a warning sign.Status questions
C Confidence"How confident are you in the date, 1 to 10? What would make it a 9?"An honest number. Under 7 means you act now, not on the due date.When it's slipping
E End with owners"So: [owner] does [next step] by [date]. I'll check on [date]."They agree with the line as you read it back.One-page tracker

Why These Build Credibility

  • They're the questions experienced leaders ask. LEAD is incident command, SAFER is a blameless postmortem, and PACE is how a good program manager runs status. You're using proven practice, in plain words.
  • They work without technical knowledge. Every letter is about ownership, impact, evidence, or dates, which is exactly where a senior manager adds value.
  • Repetition makes you predictable. When your team knows you'll ask "what's the Effect?" or "what's your Confidence?", they start bringing the answers before you ask.
