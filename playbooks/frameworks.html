Reliability Field Guide

Pocket Frameworks

Three one-word frameworks you can grab under pressure: LEAD for incidents, SAFER for postmortems and RCAs, and PACE for tracking people's work.

One Rule Behind All of These: Own Decisions, Not Work

You own decisions, escalations, exec comms, and naming owners. Investigation, fixes, chasing, and write-ups get a named owner, usually not you. Before you say "I'll…", ask: is this only something I can do? If not, name an owner. Questions that keep it with the owner.

How to Use These

Each letter is one question you ask out loud. You don't need the technical answer; you need the next question. The "Sounds like" column is taken from one realistic week where all of these get used, messily. Print this page, or keep it open on a second screen.

Why These Build Credibility

  • They're the questions experienced leaders ask. LEAD is incident command, SAFER is a blameless postmortem, and PACE is how a good program manager runs status, in plain words.
  • They work without technical knowledge, and without authority. Every letter is about ownership, impact, evidence, or dates.
  • Repetition makes you predictable. When your team knows you'll ask "what's your Confidence?", they start bringing the answer before you ask.
