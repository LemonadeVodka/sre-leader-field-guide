Use This When

You're about to walk into a technical conversation and you won't be the most technical person in the room. You don't have to be. A sharp question shows judgment, and judgment is what you're there for.

How to Use a Question Well
  • Bring three, not ten. Pick the ones that fit this meeting. Meeting Prep does this for you.
  • Ask, then go quiet. Let the silence do the work. Don't rescue them with a multiple-choice answer.
  • Listen for the weak answer, not the jargon. Vague, "it depends" with no follow-up, "the team," "soon": those are your follow-up cues.
  • Turn every good answer into a commitment if it includes an action.
