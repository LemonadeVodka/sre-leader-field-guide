Incident

LEAD, then every 15 minutes

  1. Who's leading this?If nobody: "[Name], can you lead until the owning team joins? I'll handle exec updates."
  2. What are users seeing, how many, since when, and is it getting worse?If they read the alert: "In plain words, what can't users do?"
  3. What's ruled out, what's our best guess, and what's the safest thing we can do in 15 minutes?If "still investigating": "Is there a rollback, failover, or flag we could use meanwhile?"
  4. Who are we waiting on, did they acknowledge in PagerDuty, and when's the next update?If "pinged in Slack": "Page them now. No ack in 10 minutes, I'll call their manager."

Every 15 min: what changed · what's next · who are we waiting on · severity still right?

Which kind of incident?

Availability Down
  1. Who's leading, and is the owning pod on the bridge?
  2. Is it everyone, or one region, app, or version?
  3. What changed in the last 24 hours, and can we roll it back?

Lever: Roll back the last deploy or config; fail over the region; turn off the newest feature flag. Usually the owning pod or platform on-call.

Exec line
Sev 1: the assistant is unavailable for [most / all] users since [time]. We're [rolling back / failing over]. Next update [time].

Error Rate Up
  1. What percentage of requests fail, and is it rising?
  2. Did anything deploy or change in the hour before it started?
  3. Which dependency do the errors point to?

Lever: Roll back the change; switch a feature flag off; fall back to a backup model or skip a failing tool.

Exec line
Sev 2: about [N]% of assistant requests are failing since [time]. Likely tied to [change / dependency]. We're [action]. Next update [time].

Slow to Start Answering (TTFT Up)
  1. How slow at p95, versus normal, and for which region?
  2. Which piece grew: edge, our prep, the model, or stream back?
  3. Did a prompt, model version, or tool change recently?

Lever: Shift traffic to a less busy region or deployment; turn off a slow tool or feature; keep more servers warm.

Exec line
Sev 2/3: the assistant is slower to start answering ([N]s vs [N]s normal) since [time]. Cause: [model load / our prep]. Next update [time].

Full case (opens in a new tab)

Slow to Finish (TTLT Up)
  1. Is it all answers, or ones that use tools or agents?
  2. Did answers get longer, or did tool calls per request go up?
  3. Is one tool or partner much slower than usual?

Lever: Cap tool calls per request; time out a slow tool and answer without it; roll back the prompt.

Exec line
Sev 3: answers are taking longer to complete ([N]s vs [N]s) since [time], mainly when [tools / agents] are used. Next update [time].

Token-Rate Errors Up (Model 429s)
  1. Which 429 is it: gateway, model, or Cosmos DB?
  2. How close to our token limit are we, and in which region?
  3. Are retries making it worse?

Lever: Shift traffic to another region or deployment; reduce retries; rate-limit the heaviest users; request more quota (slow).

Exec line
Sev 2: we're hitting the model provider's capacity limit in [region], so about [N]% of requests fail at peak. We're [shifting traffic / reducing load]. Next update [time].

Gateway 429s (Our Own Limits)
  1. Did a gateway policy change recently?
  2. Is it a few heavy users, or many?
  3. Is a client version sending too many requests?

Lever: Roll back the policy; raise the limit temporarily; block the misbehaving client version.

Exec line
Sev 3: some users are being rate-limited by our own gateway since [time] after [change]. We're [rolling back]. Next update [time].

Cosmos DB Throttling
  1. Is normalized RU consumption near 100%?
  2. Is one partition hot?
  3. Did a change increase how much we read per request?

Lever: Raise RU capacity (autoscale); roll back the change; reduce history loaded per request.

Exec line
Sev 2/3: conversation history is slow or failing to load at peak because our database is at capacity. We're [raising capacity / rolling back]. Next update [time].

CDN or Edge Errors
  1. Is it slow or failing at the edge, or behind it?
  2. Did caching, WAF rules, or routing change?
  3. Do certificates or DNS expire around now?

Lever: Roll back the WAF or routing change; renew the certificate; fail over the origin; raise the streaming timeout.

Exec line
Sev [x]: some users can't reach the assistant [or answers are cut off] due to an edge configuration issue since [time]. Fix: [action]. Next update [time].

One Region Down
  1. How many users does that region serve?
  2. Can we fail over now, and who approves it?
  3. Is the provider's or Azure's status page reporting it?

Lever: Fail over to another region; accept slower responses in the meantime.

Exec line
Sev 2: users in [region] can't use the assistant since [time]. We're failing over to [region]; they may see slower responses. Next update [time].

Sudden 401/403 Spike
  1. Did it start at a round time, like midnight?
  2. What expired, or what permission changed?
  3. Is it all users, or one feature or partner?

Lever: Rotate or renew the expired key or certificate; roll back the permission change; reconnect the partner.

Exec line
Sev 2: [feature] is failing with sign-in errors since [time], likely an expired credential. We're renewing it. Next update [time].

Safety Filter Spike
  1. How many requests are blocked, versus normal?
  2. Did a filter, prompt, or model setting change?
  3. Are the blocked requests actually harmless?

Lever: Roll back the setting change (with the Responsible AI owner's agreement).

Exec line
Sev 3: some normal requests are being refused by our safety filters since [time]. We're reviewing [the change] with the responsible AI team. Next update [time].

Quality Regression
  1. What's changed in thumbs-down or complaints, and since when?
  2. Did the model, prompt, or index change?
  3. Can we compare a few bad answers before and after?

Lever: Roll back the prompt or model version; switch off the flag; rebuild the index.

Exec line
Sev 3: we're seeing lower answer quality since [time], likely from [change]. We're rolling it back. Next update [time].

Partner or iPaaS Failure
  1. Which partner or action, and how many users?
  2. Did a connection expire, or did the partner change something?
  3. Who do we contact at the partner, and what does our contract say?

Lever: Reconnect; pause the feature and say 'partner unavailable'; contact the partner.

Exec line
Sev 3: [partner feature] isn't completing since [time] due to [partner issue]. We've paused it and contacted [partner]. Next update [time].

Token Cost Spike
  1. How much above normal, and since when?
  2. Did prompts, context, or tool calls per request grow?
  3. Is it a few users or clients, or everyone?

Lever: Roll back the change; cap tool calls; rate-limit abusive clients; set a spend alert.

Exec line
Cost alert: token spend is [N]x normal since [time], driven by [cause]. Users aren't affected yet. We're [action]. I'll confirm by [time].

On-Device Model or Update Failures
  1. Which app or model versions, and which devices?
  2. Can we pause the rollout?
  3. Is the CDN serving the files, and what's the cache hit ratio?

Lever: Pause or roll back the staged rollout; fix CDN caching; fall back to the cloud path.

Exec line
Sev 2/3: [some] users on [version] can't [download / use] [feature] since [time]. We've paused the rollout. Next update [time].

Telemetry Gap
  1. Is the system down, or just our view of it? What do users and support say?
  2. Is ingestion working, and did instrumentation change?
  3. What other signal can we use meanwhile: support tickets, synthetic checks, Pendo?

Lever: Use backup signals (support, synthetic tests); fix ingestion; roll back instrumentation changes.

Exec line
We've lost part of our monitoring since [time]. Users [are / aren't] affected as far as we can tell from [backup signal]. Next update [time].

How is it stuck?

  • Nobody owns it: "Who's incident commander right now?"
  • Impact unclear: "What are users seeing, in one sentence?"
  • Going in circles: "What have we ruled out, and how?"
  • No theory: "If you had to bet, where is it?"
  • Waiting on someone: "Who exactly, when, and did they acknowledge?"
  • Afraid to act: "What's worse: acting and being wrong, or waiting?"
  • The NOC is lost: "In your own words, what have you seen?"
  • Which piece is slow: the edge, our prep, the model, or the stream back?

4. Resolved

When they ask…

  • "When will it be fixed?" → "I can't give a reliable ETA yet. Decision on [next step] by [time]; I'll update you then."
  • "Why did this happen?" → "What we know so far is [fact]. The full why comes from the postmortem."
  • "Whose fault is it?" → "We'll look at what our systems allowed, blamelessly. Right now we're fixing it."
  • "Is it fixed?" → "Mitigated: users are OK. Resolved after [watch period]."
  • "Will it happen again?" → "Not the same way if the fixes land. Owned, dated actions within a week."
  • "Should we tell customers?" → "Here's the impact. [Support / comms] decides; I've sent them the facts."
  • "Do you need more people?" → "Yes: [team], because [reason]." or "No, the right people are on it."
  • "Why didn't SRE catch it?" → "We detected it at [time]. Fixing needed [team]'s access; that's a gap I'll raise."

Projects

PACE, for each owner

  1. What moved since last time?If "working on it": "What's the next thing that will be done, and when?"
  2. What's blocking you, or who are you waiting on?If "waiting on them": "Who exactly, since when, and what did you ask for?"
  3. How confident are you in the date, 1 to 10?If under 7: "What would make it a 9?"
  4. So: [owner] does [next step] by [date]. I'll check on [date].

"I'm waiting on…"

  • A person: "Who exactly, since when, and what did you ask for?"
  • A decision: "Whose decision is it? Let's get it on their calendar this week."
  • Access or approval: "What's the ticket, and who approves it?"
  • Another team: "Is it on their board, with a date?"
  • Information: "What's the smallest assumption we can make and move on?"
  • A vendor: "What's their response time in the contract?"
  • Always: "While you wait, what can you move forward today?"
  • Always: "Can we split it so the part that doesn't depend on them ships now?"

Open and hold the room

  • Opening: "We have [30] minutes. The one decision we need is [X]. Anything that must be covered?"
  • Rambling: "Let me pause you. What's the one thing you need from this group?"
  • Tangent: "Important. Let's park it and give it time. Who owns the follow-up?"
  • Quiet person: "[Name], you've been close to this. What are we missing?"
  • Blame: "Let's look at what the system allowed, not who. What made that easy to get wrong?"
  • No decision: "Who's the decision-maker? If not today, what do you need, and by when?"
  • Handed the work: "Happy to make the call. Who owns the work?"
  • Close: "Five minutes left. What did we decide, who owns what, by when?"
Ground rules: postmortem
Postmortem ground rules
1. Blameless: how our system let this happen, not who.
2. Facts first; if not sure, say so.
3. We leave with a few actions: one owner, a date, what done means.
Ground rules: incident bridge
Bridge ground rules
1. [Name] is incident commander; the IC decides.
2. One conversation at a time; side chats in a thread.
3. Say what you'll change before changing it.
4. I'm handling exec updates every 15 minutes.
Ground rules: SRE Daily and perf meeting
Daily and perf ground rules
Daily: decisions and blockers, not status; over 2 minutes goes to the parking lot; end on time.
Perf: start with the user-facing number; every regression gets a cause and an owner; leave with one owned fix.

Postmortems

  • PREP: Pick (author, date) · Request (invite, draft, NOC timeline) · Evidence (check the draft) · Pre-wire
  • SAFER: Set the rules · Agree the impact · Follow the timeline · Explain what let it happen · Rank the actions
  • CLOSE: Capture notes · Log actions · Own the follow-up · Share · End it at 30 days
