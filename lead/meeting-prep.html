Meeting Prep

Use This When

You have five minutes before a pod sync, design review, post-incident review, or boss 1:1, and you want to walk in prepared and walk out with something written down.

Example in Practice

Two minutes before the Assistant pod sync. You pick "Pod or Peer Sync," type "Assistant pod" as the title, and the tool shows two open items from last week's sync. One has already moved twice. You tick three questions, including "This issue paged us 31 times last month. Who owns fixing it, and by when?"

In the meeting, you ask about the overdue item first, then your three questions. You log two commitments as they're said. You hit Save, the recap is copied, and you paste it into the meeting chat before anyone leaves. Next week, those items are waiting for you in "Check These First."

What Actually Happens
The meeting runs long on someone else's topic and you never get to your questions.
Counter: ask your #1 question in the first five minutes. "Before we dive in, one quick one…"
People remember the meeting differently afterwards.
Counter: the recap within the hour is your record. Ending with "if I've got anything wrong, reply" makes silence agreement.
You prep, but the other side didn't, so everything is "let me get back to you."
Counter: log "get back to me" as a commitment with a date. It is one.
