Commitment Tracker

Use This When

Someone says "I'll look into it," "should be done next week," or "we'll get to that after the launch." If it isn't written down with a name and a date, it isn't a commitment. It's a wish.

Add a Commitment

If you leave the follow-up blank, it's set to 2 days before the due date. Moving a due date later records a slip, so patterns show up.

Pick a Message, Copy, Send

Edit the wording to sound like you. Send it where they actually read messages.

Ask Like a TPM: The Six Questions

You don't need to own the project plan. You need six answers before a commitment counts.

AskWhySay it like this
Owner"The team" means nobody"Who's the one person I check with?"
Date"Soon" isn't a date"What would have to be true to commit to Friday?"
Done MeansStops the "it's done" vs "it's not fixed" argument"What will we be able to see when it's done?"
DependenciesMost slips are waiting on someone else"What are you waiting on, and from whom?"
RisksGets bad news early"What's most likely to knock this off track?"
Next CheckpointYou find out about a slip mid-way, not on the due date"When's the earliest we'd know it's slipping?"
Example in Practice

Pod sync. The pod lead says: "Yeah, we know about the timeout alerts, we'll look into it."

You: "Thanks. Who's the one person I should check with? … Great, Priya. What would 'done' look like, so we're not guessing? … Fewer than 5 pages a week? Perfect. Is the end of next week realistic, or what's in the way? … OK, waiting on access from the platform pod. I'll chase that one. Let's check in Wednesday."

You then log it here: Fix assistant timeout alerts · Priya · due Fri · done = under 5 pages a week for 2 weeks · waiting on platform access (you own that chase). In other words, two minutes of questions turned "we'll look into it" into something you can track.

What Actually Happens
They agree in the meeting, then it never comes up again.
Counter: send a two-line recap within an hour ("Confirming: Priya, timeout alerts, next Friday, done = under 5 pages a week"). A written recap is hard to forget.
The date keeps moving and the status stays "on track."
Counter: the tracker counts slips. At two, stop nudging the owner and use the "reset the conversation" message. At three, it's a priority conflict for their manager, not a motivation problem.
They mark it done, but nothing changed.
Counter: that's why "Done Means" has to be checkable. Reopen it and point to the definition you both agreed on.
Some people will see tracking as micromanagement.
Counter: track outcomes and dates, never hours or methods. Say it plainly: "I track it so I can unblock you and take heat off you, not to check up on you."
Do It This Week
  • Log every commitment from your meetings this week, including ones made to you and ones you made.
  • Send a written recap after your next pod sync.
  • On Friday, close the loop: every item gets marked done, slipped, or dropped.
