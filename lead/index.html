Lead Daily

Most of leading SRE isn't technical. It's getting the right people to commit to the right things, following up without nagging, and making sure nothing important dies in a meeting. These tools do the bookkeeping, so you can focus on the conversations.

Tools

Tool

Commitment Tracker

Who owes what, by when, and what "done" means. Counts date slips and writes your nudge messages.

 Tool

Meeting Prep

The right questions and open items for this meeting. Notes become commitments with one click, and a recap gets copied.

How the Pieces Connect

  1. Before a meeting: Meeting Prep shows open commitments for those people and suggests questions.
  2. During: anything someone agrees to do becomes a Commitment line.
  3. After: save, and the recap is copied for the chat. Commitments go to the Tracker with follow-up dates.
  4. Every morning: Today shows what's overdue and who to follow up with.
  5. Friday: close the loop and log wins.
