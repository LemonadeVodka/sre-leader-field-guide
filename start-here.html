Your First 15 Minutes

  1. Read Your Path: IT Ops → SRE Leader (8 min). It reframes your background as an advantage and sets the bar for what you need to know.
  2. Rate yourself on the Skills Map (5 min). Rate quickly and honestly. Your weakest skill becomes this week's focus.
  3. Log three open commitments in the Commitment Tracker (2 min). Pick things people told you they'd do this week.

Tomorrow morning, open Today and work the checklist.

Your Data

The Rules
  • Everything you type stays in this browser (localStorage). Nothing is sent anywhere. There are no analytics and no accounts. The site is public; your notes aren't.
  • Pick one main browser for the tools, such as your work laptop. Data doesn't sync between browsers or devices on its own.
  • Back up weekly. Today reminds you after 7 days. Clearing browser data, or some company browser policies, will erase it.
  • To move devices: back up here, then restore the file on the other device.
  • Think before you type names. Commitments and notes about people are sensitive. Keep entries factual, and follow your company's policy on work notes.
